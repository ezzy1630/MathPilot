# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MathPilot is for a single local macOS user studying Calculus 1 and Calculus 2 who wants directed practice, diagnosis, review, and homework help without managing a school-style dashboard. The user opens the app to find the next best calculus action, complete active work, repair weak skills, and keep local progress reliable.

## Product Purpose

MathPilot is a private, local-first calculus mastery engine. It owns structured learning state, recommends what to do next, teaches through targeted resources and explanations, checks math beyond exact string matching, updates mastery and review queues, and improves its local teaching system over time.

## Brand Personality

Serious, clear, calm, and motivating without childish game language. The product should feel like an Apple-style productivity app combined with an ALEKS-like mastery map and light, tasteful learning momentum.

## Anti-references

Do not make MathPilot feel like a generic chatbot wrapper, LMS, quiz toy, video playlist, cluttered analytics dashboard, childish gamified app, or AI-generated SaaS surface. Avoid corny reward language, generic card grids, decorative dashboards full of chores, and UI that hides the next action behind navigation.

## Design Principles

- One action first: opening the app should answer what to do right now.
- Complexity is earned: advanced plans, analytics, maps, and developer details stay available but secondary.
- Math work feels active: the activity workspace prioritizes solving, feedback, hints, examples, and repair over passive watching.
- Trust comes from structure: mastery, review, homework analysis, AI help, and maintenance are visible enough to be inspectable without becoming busy.
- Motivation stays adult: progress is acknowledged through precise learning language, not game noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility for all primary flows. Support reduced motion, clear focus states, readable math input, non-color-only mastery state indicators, and local-first operation without a required cloud account.
